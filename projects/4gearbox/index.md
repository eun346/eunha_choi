# Gearbox Design and Motion Study

## Overview

This MEC 203 project focused on designing and assembling a multi-component gearbox using Autodesk Fusion 360. The goal was to create individual mechanical components, assemble them into a functional mechanical arrangement, and visualize gear movement through a motion study.

The final CAD assembly includes spur gears, three shafts, bearings, and a supporting housing.

## My Contribution

I independently modeled the gearbox components, assembled the mechanical system, prepared a technical assembly drawing, and created a motion study in Fusion 360.

I also worked on resolving component alignment issues, particularly between the gears and shafts.

## Technologies and Tools

- **Autodesk Fusion 360:** 3D part modeling, assembly design, and motion visualization.
- **CAD Modeling:** Creation of the housing, shafts, and gears with different dimensions and geometries.
- **Mechanical Assembly:** Positioning and alignment of gears, shafts, bearings, and housing components.
- **Technical Drawing:** Assembly drawing with component labels and a parts list.
- **Motion Study:** Visualization of the gear system's movement.

## Implementation

1. **Component Modeling:** Created individual CAD models of the gearbox housing, three shafts, and spur gears with different tooth counts, hole diameters, and thicknesses.
2. **Assembly:** Combined the modeled components with standard parts, including bearings, and positioned them within the housing.
3. **Technical Drawing:** Produced an assembly drawing showing the complete gearbox, multiple views, component labels, and a parts list.
4. **Motion Study:** Created an animation in Fusion 360 to visualize the intended movement of the assembled gears.

## Technical Challenges

One of the main challenges was accurately aligning the gears and shafts during assembly.

My initial approach involved positioning the shafts and bearings first, then moving the gears onto the shafts. However, this method made precise alignment difficult.

To address this issue, I changed the assembly process by positioning the gears first and creating the shafts based on their locations.

This revised approach made component placement and alignment more manageable.

## Results and Demonstration

The project resulted in a completed CAD gearbox assembly, a technical assembly drawing, and a motion study.

The assembly drawing documents the arrangement of the gears, shafts, bearings, and housing, while the motion study visualizes their intended rotational movement.

The project demonstrates mechanical CAD modeling, assembly design, and problem-solving through iterative component alignment. It was a CAD-based project rather than a physically manufactured or experimentally tested gearbox.
